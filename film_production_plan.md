# Film Production Coordination Plan (2026‑2029)

**Purpose**: Provide a product‑manager‑level roadmap for scheduling the nine short‑film projects, allocating set‑design, cinematography, sound, talent, and post‑production resources across four countries (Germany, Austria, Italy, Greece) over the next three years.

---

## 1. Overview

| Year | Films | Primary Countries | Notable Themes |
|------|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| **2026** | 3 | Germany, Austria | Urban‑myth, Alpine folklore, Tech‑noir |
| **2027** | 3 | Italy, Greece | Mediterranean noir, Classical myth reinterpretation |
| **2028** | 3 | Germany, Italy, Greece | Cross‑border contemporary drama |

Each film runs a **12‑week production cycle** (pre‑proc 4w, shoot 4w, post‑proc 4w) with a 2‑week buffer for travel and hand‑off. The schedule staggers start dates to keep core crews partially overlapping while allowing remote specialists (sound designers, editors) to move between projects.

## 4. Coordination Tools & Processes

| Area | Tool | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| **Scheduling** | Airtable / Smartsheet | Master production calendar, resource Gantt view |
| **Communication** | Slack (dedicated #film‑production channel) + Zoom | Real‑time updates, daily stand‑ups |
| **Document Sharing** | Google Drive / Notion | Scripts, storyboards, contracts, asset repo |
| **Asset Management** | Frame.io (cloud) | Video dailies, review & approval workflow |
| **Budget Tracking** | QuickBooks Online | Cost control per film, travel expenses |
| **Risk Management** | Confluence page | Issue log, mitigation plans |

**Standard Process** (per film):
1. **Kick‑off (Week 0)** – 2‑hour video call, finalize script, assign leads.
2. **Pre‑Production (Weeks 1‑4)** – Set design mock‑ups, location permits, casting.
3. **Production (Weeks 5‑8)** – On‑site shoot, daily dailies uploaded to Frame.io.
4. **Post‑Production (Weeks 9‑12)** – Remote sound, editing, color‑grade.
5. **Wrap‑Up (Week 13‑14)** – Deliverables, debrief, archive assets.

---

## 5. Risk & Contingency Planning

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| Weather delays (outdoor shoots) | Medium | High | Reserve indoor backup locations; schedule buffer weeks |
| Visa/Travel restrictions | Low | Medium | Early travel paperwork; remote‑first crew for restricted regions |
| Remote team bandwidth issues | Medium | Medium | Dual‑cloud rendering; redundant editor assignments |
| Budget overruns | Low | High | Quarterly budget review, contingency fund (10 % of each film) |
